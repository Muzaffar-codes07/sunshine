# Gaps

**No feature gap is proven.** We can only say a capability didn't appear in the public pages we read, so rivals may have it. Each gap below is labelled honestly:

- **Process problem:** the problem is documented in published sources.
- **Unknown competitor feature:** it didn't appear on the pages we read.
- **Opportunity hypothesis:** we think it would help, but nobody has tested it.

## Odoo-specific gap

Since this is an Odoo event, verify the current edition's serial-warranty workflow rather than claiming a weakness from an old OCA add-on or forum workaround. Those sources do not establish a current product limitation. Odoo's solar page doesn't mention PM Surya Ghar; that leaves its support unknown, rather than absent.

## What would prove or kill these

Spending 30 minutes on a SolarBuddy or Solar Ladder trial or demo would test G1, G2, G5 and G8 directly. Both advertise free trials or demos, but signing up is your call, so I didn't.

Interviewing even two or three installers about a recent disputed repair would test G1 to G4. It's the single most useful check, and hasn't been done.
