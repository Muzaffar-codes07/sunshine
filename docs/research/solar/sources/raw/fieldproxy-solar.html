Questions

Straight answers.

What is the best software for a solar business?

+

The best solar software is the one that fits how your business actually runs: site surveys, permits and interconnection, installs, commissioning, and the O&M and service work that follows. Design tools handle the system layout; you still need software to run the project and the crews. Fieldproxy is solar software set up to your own stages and checklists, with AI agents that handle scheduling, customer updates and invoicing, one price per company with unlimited users.

How much does solar software cost?

+

Per-user tools often start low and rise with every installer and office hire. Fieldproxy is one price per company with unlimited users, so adding crews, electricians and office staff does not add cost.

Can it track permits, interconnection and PTO?

+

Yes. Each project moves through your own stages, such as survey, design, permit, install, inspection, commissioning and PTO. Every stage has an owner, dates and the documents it needs, and the AI flags any project that has sat in one stage too long.

How does the handover from install to O&M work?

+

Commissioning closes with readings and photos, and the panels, inverters and batteries on the job become assets with serial numbers and warranty dates. Service plans, inspections and repair calls then run against those assets for the life of the system.

How long does it take to switch from Jobber, simPRO or spreadsheets?

+

We map how you run on a 30-minute call, configure Fieldproxy to it within 48 hours, then migrate your customers, sites, projects and service plans. Fieldproxy AI moves it all over from any major tool or custom system in less than a day, and you switch over when your crews are ready.

Is there AI for solar businesses?

+

Fieldproxy's AI agents answer calls, texts and emails, book surveys, schedule installs and inspections, keep customers updated at every stage, chase missing permit documents and photos, and draft invoices. They work inside your own solar workflow, and you confirm anything that matters before it happens.
