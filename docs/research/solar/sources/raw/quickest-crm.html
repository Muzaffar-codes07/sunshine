What is a solar CRM?

A solar CRM is customer relationship management software shaped around how solar is actually sold and installed, instead of a general sales tool with solar-sounding labels bolted on. The difference shows up in three places: the pipeline stages, the maths, and the paperwork.

A generic CRM gives you stages like Lead, Qualified, Proposal, Won. A solar deal does not work that way. It runs enquiry, site survey, quotation, subsidy application, net metering, installation, then operations and maintenance. Two of those stages sit with a government portal and a DISCOM rather than with your sales rep, and a CRM that cannot represent them leaves your team guessing where a deal actually is.

A solar CRM also has to do arithmetic that a general CRM has never heard of. System sizing against a customer's consumption. Bill of materials with GST. Payback period. And in India, the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana subsidy, which changes what the customer pays and is the single number most likely to be got wrong in a hurry on a rooftop.

Why a general CRM fails for solar businesses

Most Indian EPCs arrive here after trying a general CRM, a spreadsheet, or both at once.

It cannot size a system

A general CRM stores a deal value you typed in. It cannot take a monthly bill and tell you the plant size, so that work goes back to a spreadsheet and stops being visible to anyone else.

It does not know the subsidy

PM Surya Ghar changes the customer's net cost. If the CRM cannot apply the current slabs, every quote is a manual calculation, and manual calculations under time pressure are where the margin goes.

It has no DISCOM stage

Net metering approval is a real stage that a real person has to chase. A pipeline without it makes signed orders look complete when they are actually waiting on a utility.

It assumes email

Indian solar customers reply on WhatsApp. A CRM built on email sequences produces follow-up that nobody reads and reporting that says you followed up when you did not.

It assumes a desk

The moment to send a quote is on the roof, while the customer is standing next to you. If the CRM is a desktop tool, the quote goes out that evening, or the next day, or not at all.

It leaves you in two systems

The common end state is a CRM holding names and a spreadsheet holding the actual solar work. Two systems, one of which is out of date, and no owner able to see the real pipeline.

The honest comparison is not really CRM against CRM. For most Indian installers it is a solar CRM against Excel plus WhatsApp, which is the incumbent worth beating.

The pipeline a solar CRM has to model

Seven stages, two of which are outside your control. A CRM that hides those two will mislead you.

1

Enquiry

Lead arrives from Meta, IndiaMART, a Google form, a referral or a walk-in. It lands in one inbox instead of four.

2

Site survey

Roof area, shading, sanctioned load, meter details. Captured on the phone, on site, with photos attached to the lead.

3

Quotation

System size, bill of materials, GST, labour. The subsidy applied automatically so the net figure is right the first time.

4

Subsidy application

PM Surya Ghar registration and documentation. Tracked as a stage, because it gates everything after it.

5

Net metering

DISCOM application, feasibility, meter installation. Owned by the utility, chased by you, visible to the owner.

6

Installation

Material dispatch, mounting, commissioning. The point at which the customer expects to hear from you daily.

7

Operations and maintenance

Service visits, warranty claims, and the referrals that come from a customer who was not abandoned after commissioning.

What a solar CRM needs to handle in India specifically

This is where an imported product struggles, however good it is at everything else.

PM Surya Ghar

The PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana subsidy is the first question a residential customer asks and the number they will hold you to. Your CRM should apply the current slabs to the system on the quote and show the customer their net payable, not send your rep back to a calculator.

DISCOM and net metering

Rules, load limits and timelines differ by state and by distribution company. A solar CRM built elsewhere treats net metering as an afterthought. In India it is a pipeline stage that decides whether a signed deal becomes a commissioned plant this quarter or next.

GST on the quotation

A solar quotation that does not carry GST correctly is a quotation you will have to reissue. The tax treatment belongs in the document the customer sees, not in a separate invoice conversation later.

WhatsApp as the channel

Email open rates are not the metric that matters here. Proposals go out on WhatsApp, follow-ups happen on WhatsApp, and approvals arrive as a voice note. A solar CRM for India has to treat that as the primary channel rather than an integration.

Solar CRM questions

What is a solar CRM?
A solar CRM is customer relationship management software built around how solar is actually sold and installed, rather than a general sales tool with solar labels on it. It tracks a lead through the stages a solar deal really has, which are enquiry, site survey, quotation, subsidy application, net metering, installation and then operations and maintenance. It also handles the calculations a solar quote needs, including system sizing, GST, and in India the PM Surya Ghar subsidy.
How is a solar CRM different from a general CRM like Zoho or HubSpot?
A general CRM stores contacts and deal stages. It has no idea what a kilowatt is, cannot size a system, cannot compute a subsidy, and does not know that a net metering application sits between a signed order and a commissioned plant. Teams using general CRMs end up doing the solar-specific work in spreadsheets alongside the CRM, which is where the errors and the lost follow-ups come from.
Does a solar CRM calculate the PM Surya Ghar subsidy?
Quickest Solar CRM does. It applies the current PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana slabs to the system size on the quote, so the customer sees the subsidy and the net payable without anyone working it out by hand. Subsidy rules change, so always confirm the current slabs against the national portal before submitting an application.
Can a solar CRM send proposals on WhatsApp?
Yes, and in India it should. Most solar buyers reply on WhatsApp rather than email. Quickest Solar CRM generates the proposal PDF and sends it on WhatsApp from the same screen, and follow-up reminders run on WhatsApp too.
Is there a free solar CRM?
Quickest Solar CRM has a free plan at zero rupees forever: 10 proposals a month, one user, no card required. Proposals on the free plan carry a watermark. The Pro plan is 6,999 rupees per user per year with a three-user minimum.
Does a solar CRM work on mobile?
It has to. Solar sales happen on rooftops and in customer living rooms, not at a desk. Quickest Solar CRM runs on iOS, Android and the web, and a proposal can be built and sent from a phone during the site visit.
How long does it take to move from Excel to a solar CRM?
Most teams send their first proposal within about 30 minutes of signing up. Moving historical leads across takes longer and depends on how your spreadsheet is organised. Start by running new leads through the CRM and migrate the back catalogue afterwards.
Does a solar CRM handle GST on quotations?
A solar CRM built for India should. Quickest Solar CRM applies GST to the bill of materials and shows it on the quotation, so the figure the customer sees is the figure they pay.
