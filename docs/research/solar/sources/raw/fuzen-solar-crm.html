What's Inside the Solar CRM Pro Template

A ready-to-use solar CRM covering Dashboard, Leads, Surveys, Quotations, Projects, Subsidy Tracker, Payments, Loans, Inventory, Reports, After-Sales and Settings, every one built around how Indian installers actually work.

Lead capture with source tracking

Capture every enquiry into a single pipeline; tag the source (referral, Google Ads, dealer, walk-in, housing society) so you know what's working.

8-stage solar sales pipeline

AI-customise

Ships with: New → Contacted → Survey Scheduled → Quote Sent → Negotiation → Won → Lost → Junk. Ask the AI builder to rename, reorder or add stages.

PM Surya Ghar-aware quotations

Pick components from your catalog; the quote auto-calculates GST and applies the right central + state subsidy slab. The customer sees their net cost after subsidy on every PDF. The same line items appear in our free solar quotation format template, so you can start by hand and switch to auto-generated quotes later.

Mobile-friendly site survey form

Capture roof area, shading, DISCOM connection and photos directly from the technician's phone. The form pre-fills customer details from the lead. Your field team can install it as a mobile app (PWA) and update projects from any phone.

Product catalog with brand + GST defaults

Seed your panels, inverters and BOS once; quote them in one click. Brand, unit, rate and GST% defaults per item.

10-milestone install tracker

Every project ships with the same 10-step checklist solar installers actually use, from material procurement to DISCOM inspection, net-meter and subsidy disbursement. Photo upload per milestone.

Email notifications and reminders

Turn on reminders for follow-ups, overdue payments, AMC renewals and subsidy chasers from Settings. Automated WhatsApp messages to customers come with Solar CRM Platinum.

Milestone-linked payments + GST invoices

Record advances, delivery payments and final balance against each project. Generate a GST-compliant invoice the moment the project is commissioned.

PM Surya Ghar tracker built in

Track every subsidy application across registration → documents submitted → approved → disbursed. Stuck applications surface on the dashboard so nothing sits longer than 45 days.

AMC + service ticket centre

Log inverter faults, panel output issues and net-meter complaints from the same system that sold the project. AMC contracts auto-remind you 30 days before expiry.

Role-tailored dashboards

Admins see pipelines and stuck subsidies; sales sees today's follow-ups; technicians see today's surveys and milestones. Four pre-built roles: Admin, Sales Executive, Field Technician, Accounts.

Loan and financing tracking

Most PM Surya Ghar customers buy on a bank loan. Track each loan against its project, from application to sanction to disbursement, next to the subsidy.

Inventory for panels, inverters and BOS

Record stock coming in, issue material to each project and see what is running low, so an install never waits on a missing inverter or cable.

Sales and collections reports

See which lead sources close, where deals stall in the pipeline and what is still owed across projects, without building a spreadsheet.
