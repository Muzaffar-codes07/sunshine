Understanding asset management:

The Asset Management module helps you track and manage customer-owned equipment or products effectively. It maintains a complete record of each asset's service and repair history and provides insights into how often maintenance is required or when breakdowns occur. With proactive maintenance of assets, you can help your customers minimize downtime, prevent unexpected breakdowns, and reduce costly repairs.

Store and access key asset information

Add customer assets into Zoho FSM along with information like Asset Number, GIAI, customer information, asset address, and purchase and installation dates. Lead customer conversations with all the details of the product they own. Make service decisions based on the asset purchase date and warranty periods.

Make informed decisions with asset histories

Get a quick rundown of all the services and maintenance activities taken up for an asset in a single screen. Access the related service requests, estimates, work orders, and service appointments to understand all the work taken up in the past and make data-backed decisions for future actions. Create new requests, estimates, and work orders directly from the Asset module to maintain context.

Handle assets having multiple components

Some equipment and machinery may contain multiple components with different Asset IDs and warranty periods. Zoho FSM lets you group these components under one asset while enabling you to capture information unique to them. You can add multiple child assets under an asset to link them and add the Asset number, GIAI, warranty expiry date, and other details for each of the child assets.

Set up periodic maintenance plans

Avoid unpleasant breakdowns and unaccounted downtime by keeping assets in top shape with periodic maintenance plans. Extend asset lifespan by scheduling regular daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly maintenance activities. Zoho FSM supports setting up recurring work orders and service appointments that trigger at your chosen interval to help you run maintenance activities without a glitch.

Frequently asked questions

An asset is any customer-owned product or machinery that may require service or maintenance. Zoho FSM lets you manage customer assets and track their condition and history to provide timely service.

You can add assets manually, import data in bulk, or integrate with other platforms. For instance, assets can be added from the Product and Sales Order module in Zoho CRM and used seamlessly in the work order management process on Zoho FSM.

Beyond Asset number, GIAI, and purchase and warranty expiration dates, you can also add notes with images, and attach documents like manuals, contracts, and service agreements for improved asset management.

Zoho FSM lets you set up periodic maintenance plans that create recurring work orders and service appointments based on the interval you choose. You can make this a part of your scheduling system to offer regular asset maintenance services.
