How a solar deal moves

The 7-stage solar sales pipeline a CRM should hold

Every Indian rooftop deal passes the same seven handoffs. If your software cannot show which stage a deal is in and who owns it, follow-up depends on memory.

See how the stages work in pipeline management, or read what a sales pipeline is.

Who it is for

Solar CRM software for EPC companies

An EPC sells, designs, procures, installs and commissions. The record has to survive each handoff, from the salesperson to the survey team to the crew on the roof.

What an EPC should demand

  • One owner per lead, and history that stays when a rep leaves.
  • Proposal versions kept together, so the document the customer agreed to is always findable.
  • Survey photos, the equipment list and approvals passed to the install team before the van is booked.
  • Residential and commercial deals tracked separately, since commercial work adds tenders and variations.

Where a focused CRM stops

Quickest covers lead to proposal to follow-up and the pipeline. It is not an inventory, purchasing or accounting system. An EPC that wants solar project management software with stock and accounts in the same place should test Odoo and plan the configuration time. See the proposal generator and the quotation system for the sales side.

Where customers reply

WhatsApp follow-up for solar installers

Many Indian solar buyers reply on WhatsApp sooner than on email. The question is whether the conversation belongs to the deal or to one salesperson's phone.

Test these in a demo

  • Is the thread attached to the deal, so a colleague can pick it up?
  • Is opt-in recorded for each contact, in line with the DPDP Act?
  • Who pays for business-initiated conversations, and are message templates approved on the WhatsApp Business API?
  • Can a rep send the proposal PDF and log the reply in the same screen?

What Quickest does here

Follow-up runs on WhatsApp with templated messages and scheduled reminders attached to the deal. Ask whether WhatsApp conversation charges are extra to the ₹6,999 seat price, and confirm consent handling, in your order form. Details on WhatsApp follow-up.

Scheme-driven selling

A solar CRM for PM Surya Ghar installers

Much Indian residential rooftop work is sold through PM Surya Ghar. A CRM cannot file the application for you, but it should hold the record the scheme needs.

What the record should hold

  • Consumer account, state and DISCOM, and feasibility status.
  • The subsidy basis and date the quote used, because scheme rules change.
  • Module and inverter models, so ALMM and DCR checks have evidence behind them.
  • Installation photos, inspection and meter stages through commissioning.

One rule for proposals

Show the subsidy as an estimate against a stated scheme version, never as guaranteed money. Quickest produces subsidy-ready PDF proposals, but you still verify the current scheme and your DISCOM's process. Background reading: the PM Surya Ghar application process and becoming an empanelled vendor.

Before the proposal

Solar lead management software

Enquiries go cold when they sit in a shared inbox or a salesperson's notebook, so lead handling comes before proposals.

What good lead management does

  • Pulls enquiries from website forms, Facebook lead ads and listing sites into one list.
  • Assigns each lead to a named rep (lead routing) and flags deals that stall.
  • Records the source, so you can see which channel produces signed deals, not just enquiries.
  • Keeps duplicates and returning customers from being treated as new.

How to check it

Import ten real leads from last month and ask who owns each, what happened next and which source it came from. If the answer takes more than a minute per lead, the tool is not doing the job. Quickest covers this in lead capture and reports it in sales reports.

Country research

An Indian solar CRM must preserve the scheme, DISCOM, equipment, tax, and handover record

An Indian rooftop record should connect the consumer account, state and DISCOM, bills, feasibility status, site survey, design, module and inverter models, proposal revision, vendor agreement, installation evidence, geo-tagged photos, inspection, metering agreement, commissioning, subsidy status, GST documents, consent, warranty, and handover. Commercial work needs separate tender, engineering, procurement, milestone, variation, and acceptance stages.

01

MNRE describes the Grid Connected Rooftop Solar Programme and links consumers to PM Surya Ghar and DISCOM portals. National policy does not erase state-level execution. Store the state, serving DISCOM, connection number, applicable portal, process version, and responsible owner for each property.

Ministry of New and Renewable Energy: Grid Connected Rooftop Solar Programme ↗
02

The official PM Surya Ghar CFA guidelines place consumer registration, vendor choice, agreed system and commercial terms, technical compliance, feasibility where applicable, installation, portal updates, DISCOM inspection, meter installation and agreement, and CFA processing into a connected journey. A CRM should preserve submissions and accepted versions rather than merely showing a subsidy stage.

Ministry of New and Renewable Energy: PM Surya Ghar CFA operational guidelines ↗
03

The guidelines say the consumer chooses a vendor registered on the National Portal and mutually agrees the system, quality, warranty, and financial terms. They also state that actual vendor agreements can vary. Keep the exact signed proposal, agreement, equipment schedule, payment terms, warranty promise, and later variation together.

Ministry of New and Renewable Energy: PM Surya Ghar CFA operational guidelines ↗
04

After installation, the guidelines call for system details and geo-tagged photographs on the portal. The application then moves to DISCOM inspection and the appropriate agreement. CFA processing follows physical verification. Store the photo source, coordinates, capture time, uploader, inspection checklist, defects, correction evidence, approval, meter record, and subsidy status.

Ministry of New and Renewable Energy: PM Surya Ghar CFA operational guidelines ↗
05

The guidelines state that central CFA is based on the rated DC capacity of installed modules and set conditions for eligible residential systems. They also allow state or union-territory support to supplement central CFA. A proposal must label the source, capacity basis, customer eligibility, application date, state top-up, and approval status rather than presenting subsidy as guaranteed cash.

Ministry of New and Renewable Energy: PM Surya Ghar CFA operational guidelines ↗
06

MNRE says ALMM List I identifies eligible module models and manufacturers for government-assisted, government-scheme, open-access, and net-metering projects, and the list is updated regularly. The page also tracks List II for cells and changing clarifications. Save the exact model, manufacturer, list, revision date, DCR evidence, serials, and reviewer used for each job.

Ministry of New and Renewable Energy: Approved List of Models and Manufacturers ↗
07

CBIC publishes GST rates by goods and services classification. A rooftop contract can contain equipment, installation, civil or electrical work, services, discounts, advances, and later changes, so one universal solar GST switch is unsafe. Have a tax professional approve classifications, place of supply, invoice fields, e-invoicing or e-way-bill requirements, credit notes, and record retention.

Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs: GST goods and services rates ↗
08

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act defines Data Fiduciary and Data Principal roles, requires notice around consent requests, describes consent as free, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous, and provides a withdrawal route. Solar CRMs holding bills, Aadhaar-related documents, property images, bank details, calls, and messages need purpose, access, processor, retention, breach, correction, erasure, and export controls.

Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 ↗

The Three-Deal Proof

Run the same three opportunities in every shortlisted CRM: one ordinary lead, one revision-heavy project, and one deal that already failed. This removes demo theatre and exposes repeated entry, stale documents, weak mobile adoption, and missing local support.

  1. 1
    Run the clean deal

    Capture a real lead, assign it, create the proposal, schedule follow-up, record acceptance, and hand it to delivery.

  2. 2
    Change the assumptions

    Change the system, equipment, price, payment route, and installation date. Identify every stale record.

  3. 3
    Recreate a failure

    Use a duplicate, missed follow-up, rejected proposal, or delayed job and ask the CRM to show what happens next.

10 requirements to verify in India

01

INR prices, GST, and accounting acceptance

Build residential, housing-society, and commercial quotes in INR. Test equipment, labour, civil work, discounts, advances, milestones, retention, credit notes, and cancellation. Have the accountant approve GST classifications, invoice output, e-invoice or e-way-bill treatment, and books integration.

02

PM Surya Ghar version and eligibility

Record scheme route, consumer category, connection identity, property, sanctioned load, module DC capacity, state support, source, effective date, and reviewer. Revise a subsidy rule without changing the accepted proposal or earlier application.

03

DISCOM-specific process

Use the serving DISCOM portal and current state regulation. Test feasibility, document requests, corrections, installation update, inspection, agreement, meter procurement and installation, commissioning, approval, and subsidy status with timestamps and owners.

04

ALMM, DCR, and equipment traceability

Validate manufacturer and exact module model against the applicable ALMM revision and DCR condition. Preserve inverter and protection data, datasheets, serial numbers, purchase record, substitution approval, installation photos, warranty, and handover.

05

Residential and commercial separation

Keep subsidy-led residential work distinct from housing-society, commercial, industrial, tender, and open-access journeys. Give commercial work decision makers, engineering reviews, procurement, milestone billing, tax deductions, variations, acceptance, and service stages.

06

Design, tariff, export, and savings assumptions

Create a traceable design from bills or interval data. Show consumption, self-use, exports, losses, degradation, dated retail and export values, outage and battery assumptions, finance inputs, and uncertainty. Keep every accepted version.

07

WhatsApp consent and ownership

Test lead consent, approved templates, service and marketing messages, opt-out, sender identity, conversation assignment, attachments, delivery state, cost, and write-back. Confirm the official WhatsApp Business route and a fallback for customers using another channel.

08

Mobile and weak-connectivity field work

At a low-signal site, open the current scope, survey, roof and electrical checklist, capture geo-tagged photos, serials, defects, notes, and signatures, then sync without duplicates. Test role removal and lost-device response.

09

DPDP and processor terms

Map data purpose and source. Review notices, consent withdrawal, permissions, audit logs, processors, subprocessors, hosting, transfers, safeguards, breaches, retention, correction, erasure, bulk export, and closure before importing live records.

10

Commissioning, warranty, and handover

Use the approved design and store inspection, agreement, meter details, safety tests, commissioning, punch-list closure, subsidy evidence, warranties, customer training, manuals, signed acceptance, service tickets, and a complete export.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions about solar CRM in India

What is the best solar CRM in India?+

Quickest is our first proposal-focused trial for a small or growing Indian solar team because it connects lead ownership, mobile proposals, follow-up, WhatsApp-oriented work, and pipeline visibility. QuickEstimate publishes this ranking and sells Quickest, so it is not independent. Choose it only after your state, DISCOM, subsidy, ALMM, GST, privacy, accounting, field, and export tests pass.

Can a solar CRM connect directly to PM Surya Ghar?+

Do not assume that a portal logo or subsidy field means direct integration. Ask the vendor to demonstrate the current authorised route, credentials, data fields, consent, submission, status retrieval, corrections, audit log, and manual fallback. The official process involves the consumer, registered vendor, National Portal, and serving DISCOM, with state-specific requirements still relevant.

Which DISCOM stages should the CRM track?+

At minimum, preserve consumer and connection identity, registration, feasibility where applicable, document versions, vendor agreement, installation, system and geo-photo upload, inspection, corrections, metering agreement, meter procurement and installation, commissioning, portal approval, and CFA status. Names and order vary by state and DISCOM, so use a dated configurable workflow.

Should a proposal show PM Surya Ghar subsidy as guaranteed?+

No. Show the scheme source and date, customer and property assumptions, module DC capacity basis, central CFA calculation, any state support, eligibility conditions, application status, and who verifies the result. Keep customer price, payable amount, financing, and expected subsidy distinct. Update the assumption without overwriting the accepted proposal.

How should a CRM store ALMM and DCR evidence?+

Store the exact manufacturer, model, relevant ALMM list, revision and check date, DCR evidence when required, datasheet, supplier, purchase record, serial numbers, substitution approval, installation photos, and warranty. A brand name alone is insufficient because eligibility attaches to applicable models, rules, project routes, and dates.

What GST rate should solar CRM software apply?+

Do not use one universal switch without tax review. A contract can combine goods, installation, civil or electrical work, services, discounts, advances, and changes. Have the installer's tax adviser approve classification, rate, place of supply, time of supply, invoice fields, e-invoicing or e-way-bill obligations, credit notes, and records for the actual contract.

Does the DPDP Act apply to solar leads and documents?+

Solar records can include contact details, electricity bills, identity documents, property images, bank information, calls, signatures, and messages. Map the lawful purpose, notice, consent or other basis, access, processors, security, retention, withdrawal, correction, erasure, breach response, and export. Confirm current commencement and rule obligations with counsel before rollout.

What should an installer test in WhatsApp integration?+

Confirm the official business account and provider, consent, templates, service and marketing categories, opt-out, assignment, delivery state, attachments, conversation charges, retention, and CRM write-back. Test a customer reply, reassignment, failed delivery, number change, deletion request, and an alternate channel. Do not treat access to a share button as full integration.
