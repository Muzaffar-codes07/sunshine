# Codex critique of Claude's independent proposal

31 October 2026. Read after both proposals were frozen and announced in thread 6.

## Agreements and useful additions

Claude correctly distinguishes installer failures from software failures, rejects unsupported absence claims, and identifies real solar-specific pain. A portable customer warranty record is a strong, modest extension. A next-owner subsidy checklist is useful without pretending to integrate government systems. Both proposals agree the wedding extra-plates variation log is the strongest wedding demonstration and that neither category is novel simply by integrating ERP functions.

My independent recommendation assumed a fresh small full-stack team. Claude introduces potentially decisive context: StockSense inventory/ledger experience. I did not have that fact in the thread. If this is confirmed team experience, solar's implementation risk falls even without code reuse. My wedding preference should not override known team strengths.

## Changes needed before a merged recommendation

1. **The 4.5/5 unserved-gap score is not established.** Scheme rules make an implementable domain scenario, but they do not establish competitors leave it unserved. [SolarBuddy](https://www.solarbuddy.in/solar-inventory-software) already claims Indian installer serial scanning and warranty-document retrieval. [Zoho FSM](https://www.zoho.com/fsm/assets.html) documents asset/warranty-aware service; Odoo has an official solar industry suite. Call this problem specificity or demo differentiation, not verified white space.
2. **Separate scheme law from the PDF.** The PDF has a delivery-triggered component warranty and free covered repair. A five-year vendor service obligation may change customer billing even after a manufacturer's warranty ends. Before making this executable or pitching statutory amounts/coverage, read the current official MNRE operational document, with scope and conditions. A seller guide is inadequate for legal/financial rules. Until verified, exclude hardcoded subsidy amounts, 15-day proof deadlines and mandatory five-year free-service claims from the committed MVP. Keep the workflow configurable and show component and service coverage as separate fields.
3. **No evidence of novelty through bankruptcy.** A customer PDF/QR handover helps retain records, but it cannot preserve access to a company-hosted portal after that company disappears. Export a self-contained PDF or JSON, and avoid implying it guarantees repairs or replaces a failed installer. Bankruptcies abroad do not prove an Indian software market gap.
4. **Do not turn the demo into two MVPs.** The solar baseline already has survey gating, kit expansion, stock dispatch, serial uniqueness, scheduling, evidence, warranty tickets and reporting. Add only a portable handover card and next-owner checklist if cheap; the proposed two-clock and subsidy quote rules can consume the validation budget.
5. **Use an explicit fallback test.** Recommend solar only if the team can model and demonstrate a stock movement and persistent serial-to-customer history immediately. If starting wholly fresh with little backend capacity, wedding remains safer. Do not wait many hours to discover this.
6. **Judging and reuse remain unknown.** Engineering depth is plausible judge fit, not an official rubric. The weighted total assumes weights we invented. Present it as a heuristic sensitivity check; remove the illusion that a 12-point difference measures objective superiority.

## Proposed merger

Conditional final recommendation: **Solar if StockSense experience is confirmed; Wedding if the team is starting from scratch or has stronger wedding access.** If Claude has direct prior-user context confirming StockSense competence, lead with Solar at moderate confidence and clearly state the dependency. Ask Tuhin/leader for the selection and whether stack/reuse constraints apply. Do not ask agents to settle that choice.

Solar demo should be one persistent case: qualified enquiry → approved survey → 3 kW quote/kit → recorded advance → unique serial dispatch → customer acceptance → month-18 covered fault → owner dashboard. Keep typed serial fallback; camera scanning is an enhancement. Show an explicit demo-date override. Show an out-of-coverage case with configured service coverage disabled, so it does not contradict any scheme obligation. Internal repair costs still exist even when customer charge is zero; profit, cash and subsidy tracking must remain distinct.

Before reporting a build complete, verify survey gating, no duplicate serial allocation, no repeated stock movement on retry, warranty boundary dates, repair-cost attribution and dashboard reconciliation. This is a proposed research recommendation and build boundary, not authorization to implement.
